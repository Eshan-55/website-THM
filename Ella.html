A little introduction

Where the mountains meet the sky.

Ella is a scenic hill-country town in Sri Lanka’s Badulla District, known for its dramatic views, tea-growing landscapes and walking trails. It is a welcoming base for travellers who want to slow down and explore nature.

Places to remember.

From iconic railway views to rewarding hikes, make time for the landscapes that make Ella special.

Nine Arch Bridge

A celebrated railway viaduct set among lush hills and tea-country scenery. Check local railway information before planning around a train crossing.

Little Adam’s Peak

A popular hill walk with broad views across the surrounding mountains and valleys. Wear suitable footwear and check weather conditions.

Ravana Falls

A striking waterfall near Ella, surrounded by greenery. Enjoy the view from safe public areas and follow local safety guidance.
